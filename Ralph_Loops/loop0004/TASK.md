## Philosophy
- Read `reports/ml_nature_plan_3.md` in full first: it is the plan this loop
  executes and §0 lists what the three earlier loops established. Then read
  the sections of `reports/ml_nature.md` your item cites, the one-page §0 of
  `reports/ml_nature_summary.md`, and `CLAUDE.md`'s blocks "What the corpus
  says about the problem itself", "The generated-ensemble campaign" and "The
  objects loop". `reports/learning_plan.md` §4 and `reports/ml_nature_summary.md`
  §9 list what is closed and must not be rebuilt.
- **A prediction is never a bound.** Nothing learned may touch
  `satisfiability/mosp_solver.py::_lower_bound` or any path that decides `k`.
  A candidate bound is checked on every certified instance, attacked by the
  extremal search, written up as a conjecture, and never used by the solver.
- **No solver default changes.** Fix variants (item 04), new heuristics
  (items 01, 02) and any flag are measured behind options that default to
  today's behaviour, with paired node counts, and *proposed* in the report.
  A new heuristic strategy may be registered in `satisfiability/heuristics.py`
  as a named option; it may not become the default of anything.
- **Every split groups by file ∪ MOSP-graph isomorphism class**
  (`learning.fingerprint.union_groups`).
- **Nodes, not seconds**; seconds beside them; a censored call is a lower
  bound, never a missing value; SAT cost is counted in conflicts.
- **State the size range every conclusion covers.** Corpus 6,376 at 9–134;
  campaign 37,800 at 10–40, 6,747 at 50–75, a 5-per-cell sample at 100; item
  07 adds one certified cell at 100. Nothing below 100 is evidence about
  125 × 125 except through a stated extrapolation.
- Every number in a report is regenerated by a command named in that report.
- Findings that rest on a handful of instances are re-certified for those
  instances (`solve_mosp_exact`, never `solutions_dir=None`) before being written.

- **Compute**: check `pgrep -f benchmarks.recertify`; if it is running, its
  cores are not yours. 32 cores total; use up to 16 here. Each iteration has
  a hard 3-hour wall-clock cap enforced by the driver; items 06 and 07 price
  their runs before starting (`learning.cost_model` for 07) and sample if the
  full run will not fit, saying so.
